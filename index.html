Search engine optimization (SEO)

When someone needs a remodel, an addition, or a new build, they search general contractor and their town. When it's storm damage or a repair, they search that too.

SEO is the monthly work after the site is live. The point is those searches reach you, not the next crew.

Bid-ready jobs and urgent repairs do not use the same words. We put the quote form front and center on the urgent searches. We give remodels, additions, and new builds their own pages.

Each month we look at what is bringing jobs and what is not. Sometimes that means rewriting a page. Sometimes it means adding one.

We do not promise first place. We keep working the searches that turn into work.

Questions we get asked

How fast can a construction website go live?
A one-page site usually takes 7 to 10 days. A six-page site or larger usually takes 10 to 21 days, depending on how quick the content and photos come in.
Is $499 really the starting price?
Yes. A single-page website starts at $499. A six-page website is $999. Anything larger is a custom quote.
Are we locked into a contract?
No. There is no contract. You get the code and every login, and you can host the site elsewhere if you ever want to.
Will our site rank on Google?
We build technical SEO into the site from day one and can keep working on SEO after launch. We do not promise rankings, because Google changes and every market is different.
Can the site show that we are licensed and insured?
Yes. Homeowners and GCs look for that. We can put your license, bonding, and insurance information, service area, and a clear path to the quote form right where people expect to find it.
How do you handle urgent repairs versus bid jobs?
Construction has two kinds of buyers: the urgent repair and the bid job like remodels, additions, and new builds. We design the site so both can find what they need, with a clear path to the form for urgent work and room for the shopped jobs where the money is.
Do you build for general contractors who run multiple trades?
Yes. Many of the construction companies we build for run several trades under one roof. We can structure the site so each service is easy to find without confusing the visitor.
